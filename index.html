01 — who it is for

From first-years to campus decision-makers.

Students make digital choices before anyone has helped them think about the trade-offs. Meanwhile, privacy conversations often jump straight to extreme threats or technical jargon.

We bring the subject back to everyday life: studying, living together, travelling, collaborating and the apps that connect it all. The same foundation can then grow into deeper sessions with lecturers, researchers, student teams and organisations.

06 — learning outcomes

What we will talk about — and what you will take away.

Every workshop balances a clear explanation with a practical exercise. You do not need to be a security expert: curiosity is the only prerequisite.

theme 01

Digital footprints

We discuss metadata, trackers, account links and “free” services. You will learn to map where your data goes and identify the most important trail to reduce.

theme 02

Phones & permissions

We discuss locks, updates, notifications, sensors and app permissions. You will learn to review a phone deliberately and create safer everyday defaults.

theme 03

Accounts & identity

We discuss passwords, passkeys, 2FA, recovery codes and aliases. You will learn to protect your key accounts and recover them without panic.

theme 04

Private communication

We discuss messaging, group chats, file sharing and cloud storage. You will learn to match the channel to the sensitivity of the information.

theme 05

AI, profiling & consent

We discuss personalisation, training data, attention and power. You will learn to ask better questions before accepting a privacy claim or a default.

theme 06

Incidents & recovery

We discuss phishing, lost devices, leaked passwords and doxxing. You will learn a simple first-response plan and how to improve it after the event.
